A New Era for Hyderabad: Pakistan’s First Shariah-Compliant Green Hotel REIT

The signing ceremony for Pakistan’s first Shariah-compliant green hotel REIT, anchored at Gohar Gateway, marks a landmark moment for Hyderabad and the country’s hospitality sector.

In this story

  1. A Visionary Partnership
  2. Why This Matters
  3. A Commitment to the Future

Published on 18 July 2026, at the signing stage. The REIT, hotel brand and timelines are subject to the partners’ announcements and regulatory approvals. This is news, not an investment offer.

Hyderabad is witnessing a historic transformation. A groundbreaking partnership has officially been formed, marking a landmark moment for the city’s development and Pakistan’s hospitality sector. The signing ceremony for Pakistan’s First Shariah-Compliant Green Hotel REIT has taken place, signaling a new chapter of innovation and sustainable growth.

A Visionary Partnership

This ambitious project is the result of a strategic collaboration between two industry leaders:

  • Gohar Group of Companies: Represented by Chairman Mr. Muhammad Hanif Gohar, whose vision for urban transformation is at the core of this initiative.
  • JS Investments: A key partner in bringing this financial and developmental milestone to fruition.

The project is proudly anchored at Gohar Gateway, a development poised to redefine the urban landscape of Hyderabad.

Why This Matters

This development is more than just a construction project; it is a catalyst for change. By introducing a multinational hotel brand to Hyderabad, the project aims to achieve several critical objectives:

  • Promoting Sustainable Tourism: By focusing on “Green” initiatives, the project aligns modern hospitality with environmental responsibility.
  • Driving Strategic Investment: The creation of a Shariah-compliant REIT offers a transparent and innovative investment vehicle, contributing to national progress.
  • Urban Transformation: Through the Gohar Gateway, the project serves as a cornerstone for the modernization of the city’s infrastructure and commercial appeal.

A Commitment to the Future

At the heart of this milestone lies a commitment to innovation, transparency, and national development. By blending global hospitality standards with local development goals, Gohar Group and JS Investments are setting a new benchmark for excellence in Pakistan.

As Hyderabad continues to evolve, projects like these demonstrate the immense potential for growth, sustainable infrastructure, and world-class investment opportunities in the region.

Stay tuned for more updates on Gohar Gateway and the future of sustainable development in Pakistan.
