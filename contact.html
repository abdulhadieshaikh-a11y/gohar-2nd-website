Questions

Frequentlyasked

How do I enquire about a project?

Use the enquiry form on any project page or on our contact page, call our UAN +92 21 111 000 061, or email info@gohargoc.com. Our team responds during office hours (Monday to Saturday, 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM).

Can I visit a project site?

Yes. Request a site visit, tell us the project and the days that suit you, and our team will call you to arrange the visit.

Are payment plans available?

Payment plans are published for Gohar Gateway, Gohar Towers, Gohar Complex & Shopping Mall, Gohar Residencia & Shopping Mall and Gohar Villas. Plans and terms change over time, so please ask our sales team for the current details.

Why are some prices not shown?

Prices and availability change, and we want you to receive accurate figures. Request current pricing through the enquiry form or by phone and our team will share the latest details.

I live overseas. Can I enquire?

Yes. Select “Overseas” as your city on the enquiry form and include an international phone number. Our team will contact you by phone or email.

Where can I download a brochure?

Official brochures are available on the project pages for 141 Broadway Heights, Gohar Golf Vista, Gohar Green Corner & Shopping Center and KN Gohar Green City. For other projects, ask our team for the latest material.

Where is your head office?

A/2, Gohar Chamber, CC Area, Shahra-e-Faisal, Near Duty Free Shop, Kathiawar Society Block 7/8, Karachi, Pakistan. Open Monday to Saturday, 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM. Sunday: closed.
